# AI-powered Seller Intelligence Platform/Dashboard

AI-powered seller intelligence dashboard designed for fashion e-commerce businesses.

## Overview

This project is being developed during a hackathon as a smart analytics platform for fashion sellers.

The system combines:

- Multi-channel product integration
- Sales analytics
- AI-generated business summaries
- Trend detection
- Smart recommendations
- Inventory and return monitoring
- Market intelligence alerts

## Main Features

### Multi-Platform Integration
Simulated integrations for:
- Trendyol
- Hepsiburada
-Amazon

### Product Monitoring & Analytics
Track product performance across multiple platforms:
- Sales performance
- Inventory levels
- Return rates
- Ratings and reviews
- Platform-based product comparison

### AI Report Summaries
The system summarizes complex sales and performance reports into simple business insights.

### Trend Engine
Detects rising fashion trends and matches them with seller products.

### Market News & Intelligence
Aggregates fashion and e-commerce related news and summarizes them into seller-focused insights and alerts.

### Smart Recommendations
Provides actionable AI suggestions such as:
- Increase stock
- Promote trending products
- Improve size charts
- Expand product categories

### Alerts & Notifications
Generates intelligent alerts for:
- Stock risks
- Trend opportunities
- Return issues
- Market news
